CivicConnect

Planning Stage

CivicConnect is a civic engagement platform designed to make U.S. legislation easier to understand and follow. It provides accessible summaries of congressional bills, including their purpose, status, sponsors, policy areas, and potential effects. Users can browse legislation by topic, search for specific bills, explore civic organizations, and create personalized congressional briefings based on policy interests and elected representatives. The platform aims to help people stay informed about government activity without needing to interpret dense legislative text.

Pectus Notebook

Planning Stage

Pectus Notebook is an educational, not-for-profit resource designed to make medically supported information about pectus excavatum easier to understand and access. Created as a Girl Scout Gold Award project, it was inspired by the founder’s own experience navigating the condition and searching for reliable information. The site covers treatment options such as the Nuss and Ravitch procedures, physical therapy, and vacuum bell therapy, with information reviewed and supported by medical professionals. Its goal is to educate and support people affected by pectus excavatum while increasing awareness of the condition.
